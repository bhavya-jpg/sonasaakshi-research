# Field interview guide

Field sites: jewellers and karigar workshops in Hamirpur and nearby towns in Himachal Pradesh first, then Bengaluru (Avenue Road, Kilari Road, Jayanagar) through KAVMS introductions or calls.

Target before the prelim submission: **5 jewellers, 5 karigars (at least 3 Bengali), 1 refiner**. Run interviews in pairs: one person asks, one takes notes. Each takes 20 to 30 minutes. Log every interview in `interview-log-template.csv` the same day.

## Consent script (read aloud in the person's language)

> We are students from [college] working on a project for Tech4Bharat 2026. We want to understand how gold given for job work is recorded and settled. We will not record your name or your shop's name. With your permission, we would like to take notes, record audio, and photograph some papers with names covered. You can stop at any time or ask us to delete anything. Is that okay?

Record a yes or no for audio, photos and quotes separately.

## Jeweller (shop owner or counter staff)

Warm-up: How many years in the trade? How many karigars do you work with now? How many custom orders are open today?

1. Walk me through the last time you gave gold to a karigar. What did you write down, and who kept it?
2. How do you and the karigar agree on wastage for a piece? Is it the same for every design?
3. Show me your last 10 issue slips, with names covered. How many came back with a number you both agreed on the first time?
4. Across all karigars last month, how many grams could you not explain? How do you know?
5. When a piece is late for a wedding, what happens? Tell me about the last time.
6. When your scale and the karigar's number differ, who wins, and how is it settled?
7. How long after delivery do you usually pay? What decides when?
8. What software do you use today, if any? What does it not do for you?
9. If a karigar had his own copy of every record, signed by both of you, would that help you or worry you? Why?
10. What would you pay each month to stop unexplained losses and late orders? What would you need to see first?

## Karigar (or workshop owner)

Warm-up: Where are you from? How long in this town? Which language is easiest for you? How many jewellers do you work for?

1. When a jeweller gives you gold, what record do you get? Do you keep your own?
2. How is the wastage allowance decided? Who decides?
3. In the last month, did any jeweller's number differ from yours? What happened?
4. How many days after you delivered your last three jobs were you paid?
5. What does waiting for payment cost you: borrowing, rent, sending money home?
6. When a deadline is too tight, what do you do?
7. If you had your own record of every job, in your language on your phone, what would you use it for?
8. Has a bank or lender ever refused you? What did they ask for that you didn't have?
9. Who would you trust to keep this record: the jeweller, your association, a neutral centre, or only yourself?

## Refiner

1. How do workshops bring you scrap, and how is its weight and purity recorded?
2. How often do jewellers and karigars disagree about how much gold was in the scrap?
3. Would a jeweller and karigar accept your weighing as neutral?

## After each interview (same day)

- Fill one row in `interview-log-template.csv`.
- Write the single most surprising thing you heard.
- Mark which of our assumptions in `research/02-market-and-industry.md` it supports or breaks.
