# Artifact checklist

Capture only with consent. Cover names, phone numbers, shop names and customer details **before** taking the photo, not afterwards in editing.

| Artifact | Why judges care | Tick |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photo of an issue slip or notebook page | Proves how records are kept today | [ ] |
| The karigar's own notebook next to the jeweller's for the same job | Shows two records disagreeing | [ ] |
| Photo of the scale display during an issue | Shows the precision needed (mg) | [ ] |
| Refiner's scrap-recovery receipt | Shows the fourth party in the loop | [ ] |
| A WhatsApp voice note chasing a deadline (with permission, voices anonymised) | Shows how deadlines are handled today | [ ] |
| Timeline of the last 3 payments to one karigar (dates only) | Quantifies "waiting weeks to be paid" | [ ] |
| One audio quote from a jeweller and one from a karigar (consented) | For the deck and the pitch | [ ] |

Store originals in a private team drive, **not** in this public repository. Only masked, consented images may be committed under `field-research/artifacts/`.
