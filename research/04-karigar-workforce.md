# The karigar workforce

## Sourced

- About **3.5 lakh Bengali gold karigars** were reported returning to West Bengal from the jewellery hubs of Tamil Nadu, Kerala and Karnataka during the 2020 lockdowns ([Retail Jeweller India](https://retailjewellerindia.com/return-of-gold-artisans-to-bengal-casts-shadow-over-crafts-future)). That shows how many work far from home in the south.
- An IIM Ahmedabad working paper describes a **new community of goldsmiths from Bengal** who now dominate informal jewellery manufacturing ([IIM-A](https://iima.ac.in:443/sites/default/files/2022-11/Gold%20governance%20and%20goldsmithery.pdf)).
- Bengaluru is named among the major manufacturing and retail hubs where West Bengal karigars work ([Indian Jeweller](https://www.indianjeweller.in/Indian-Jewellery-News/12506/karigars-have-nowhere-to-return)).
- An IIM-A study for IGPC looks at trust-based informal employment and the karigar lifecycle in gold jewellery manufacturing ([IIM-A IGPC report](https://www.iima.ac.in/sites/default/files/2024-04/IGPC%20funded%20project%20BVIIMAJKJXLRI-%20Final%20Report%20June2023%20%281%29.pdf)).
- Karigar associations exist in big hubs, and pay was historically in cash ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/2017/10/09/fall-demand-mumbai-jewellery-workforce)).

## Design implications

| Reality | Design choice |
| --- | --- |
| Jeweller speaks Kannada-English; karigar speaks Bengali or Hindi | Each party hears every record in his own language; the system of record is language-neutral (numbers, units, IDs) |
| Karigar works for several jewellers | One karigar identity, many jeweller relationships; each jeweller sees only his own jobs |
| Karigar has less power | Karigar can raise a dispute, which freezes settlement until resolved; neutral re-weigh option |
| Migrant workers often lack local paperwork | Minimal KYC; assisted onboarding at a JanAI Cafe only when payout or a lender share needs it |
| Pay tied to weight lost | Settlement shows labour as both grams and rupees, read aloud |
