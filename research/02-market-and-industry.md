## What this means for us

1. **The stakes per gram are high.** At Rs 12,615/g, a 0.1 g disagreement is worth about Rs 1,260. That's enough to sour a relationship, and it's why accuracy has to hold at milligram level.
2. **Karigars are the majority of manufacturing,** so a karigar-side tool touches most of the supply chain, not a niche.
3. **Hallmarking has already pushed jewellers onto digital records,** so a jeweller is used to compliance software and is a realistic paying customer.
4. **Karigar pay is a function of weight.** A weight dispute is a pay dispute, so a fair reconciliation is also a fair wage.
